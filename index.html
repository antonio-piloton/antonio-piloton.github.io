About Me

Bridging higher education instruction, practical software architecture, and institutional innovation in the Philippine state university context.

I am an Information Technology educator, researcher, and software developer serving as an Assistant Professor III at Agusan del Sur State University (ADSSU). My professional commitment centers on equipping computing students with industry-relevant engineering competencies while designing systems that solve real administrative and operational challenges in higher education.

“Empowering next-generation technologists through rigorous computing education and pragmatic systems development.”

— Antonio Jr. Piloton

Originally hailing from Sta. Cruz, Tubajon, Dinagat Islands, I graduated with a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) from Surigao State College of Technology (now Surigao del Norte State University - SNSU) in 2020. I subsequently earned my Master in Information Technology (MIT) from SNSU in 2023, where I was conferred the honor of Best Capstone Project Awardee.

I have completed all academic requirements (CAR) in the Doctor of Information Technology (DIT) program at St. Paul University Philippines (SPUP) in Tuguegarao City. Beyond the lecture hall, I am an active software developer with a hands-on foundation in modern web frameworks (PHP/Laravel), mobile application environments (React Native with offline-first SQLite synchronization), relational databases (MySQL), and systems analysis.
